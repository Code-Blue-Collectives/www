Privacy Policy for Animal Escape Run 


Last Updated: 28-May-2026 

Code Blue Collectives ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Facebook Instant Game Animal Escape Run (the "Game"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. 

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you play our Game on the Facebook platform. 

1. Information We Collect 
Our Game is built using the Phaser framework and is designed to run entirely within your web browser or Facebook app. 
  • Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or share any personal data, such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.
  • Facebook Data: We do not access or store your Facebook profile data, friend lists, or private information.
  • Gameplay Data: Any gameplay data (such as high scores or current levels) is processed locally on your device or temporary session memory and is not saved on external servers. 

2. How We Use Information 
Since we do not collect any personal data, we do not use, sell, rent, or share your information with any third parties. 

3. Data Deletion (Facebook Requirement) 
Because our Game does not store or process any of your personal data on external servers, there is no personal data for us to delete. If you wish to remove the Game from your Facebook account, you can do so at any time through your Facebook settings: 
  1. Go to your Facebook Settings & Privacy.
  2. Click on Apps and Websites.
  3. Find Animal Escape Run and click Remove. 

4. Children's Privacy 
Our Game does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from anyone, including children. 

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy 
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.
